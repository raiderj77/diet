If it's protein + non-starchy veg, it's a yes. You don't have to ask.

The one rule

Protein and veg = always yes. Fruit = yes, ~2/day, with protein. Carbs (rice, bread, pasta, potato) are the dial: small, daytime, paired with protein. Liquid sugar and fried stuff are the only real no's.
